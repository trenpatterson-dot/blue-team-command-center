# A Cybersecurity Lab Explanation for 10-Year-Olds

In this cybersecurity lab, we played a pretend game called an Incident Response Exercise. This means we acted like real security analysts to solve a problem just like they do in a company's Security Operations Center (SOC).

We used a tool called PowerShell to help us investigate. Here's what we did:

1. We started by reading the incident summary, which told us about a pretend cyber attack.
2. Next, we figured out who was affected and what device they were using.
3. Then, we looked at the event timeline to see when things happened during the attack.
4. Lastly, we collected evidence that showed something suspicious had occurred.

By doing this lab, we learned how to investigate a cyber attack like real security analysts do. This is important because it helps us be prepared if a real attack happens and know what to do to keep our computers safe!

Unfortunately, there were some parts of the lab that we couldn't complete fully. We didn't find any screenshots to help us see what was happening during the attack, and we didn't have all the details about the commands used in the investigation. But even without those things, we still learned a lot!
