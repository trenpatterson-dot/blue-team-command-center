# Insider Risk Analysis

## Analyst Position

Insider-risk alerts require careful handling. A high-severity alert is a signal for review, not proof of malicious intent or data theft.

## Reviewed Scenario

The simulated alert involved possible transfer of sensitive business information to personal cloud storage. The review compared the alert context against local activity evidence and process observations.

## Assessment

| Area | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Sensitive data exposure | Not confirmed in available simulated evidence |
| Personal cloud storage risk | Present as an alert concern, but not confirmed as malicious transfer |
| Process activity | No malicious or unauthorized transfer utility identified |
| User context | Reviewed for training purposes only |
| Final risk | Low based on available simulated evidence |

## Analyst Cautions

- Do not accuse a user based on alert severity alone.
- Preserve privacy and need-to-know boundaries.
- Escalate only when evidence supports it.
- Keep legal, HR, and management actions with authorized owners.

## Conclusion

The simulated alert was reviewed and did not establish confirmed data exfiltration. The case demonstrates evidence-based insider-risk triage and restrained escalation.
