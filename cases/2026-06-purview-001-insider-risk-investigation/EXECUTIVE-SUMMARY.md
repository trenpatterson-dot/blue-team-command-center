# Executive Summary: Purview Insider Risk Investigation

## Summary

A simulated Microsoft Purview insider-risk alert was reviewed for possible sensitive data transfer to personal cloud storage. The available evidence did not confirm malicious activity or data exfiltration.

The case demonstrates cautious alert review, DLP reasoning, and clear separation between technical evidence and personnel or legal decisions.

## Key Points

- Alert severity alone is not proof of misconduct.
- Sensitive data exposure was not confirmed in the available simulated evidence.
- No obvious unauthorized transfer tooling was identified in the process review.
- DLP coverage, file classification, destination context, and escalation workflow should be reviewed.

## Recommendation

Close the simulated alert as low risk based on available evidence, while recommending DLP and governance improvements for future readiness.

## Evidence Boundary

This is a portfolio simulation. It does not represent live Purview access, real customer data, legal review, HR action, or production remediation.
