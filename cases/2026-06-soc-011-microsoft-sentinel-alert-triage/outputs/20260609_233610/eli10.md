# A Cybersecurity Lab Explanation for 10-Year-Olds

In this lab, we're playing detectives in a digital world! We call it a **Cybersecurity Lab**. This lab is called the "2026 06 Soc 011 Microsoft Sentinel Alert Triage". It's like a mystery story where we use special tools to solve problems.

In this lab, we used a tool called Microsoft Sentinel. It helps us keep an eye on what happens in our digital world. We looked at some signs that might mean someone was trying to break into a computer account.

Here's what we found:
1. We saw a special alert about possible trouble. This alert told us about failed attempts to sign into a computer account.
2. We checked the logs (like a diary) of these sign-in attempts. They showed that someone kept trying to get into the same account, but they couldn't!
3. We also found out who the account belonged to and where the sign-in attempts were coming from. It looked like they were coming from a computer in another place (a suspicious source IP).
4. Lastly, we checked if anyone managed to get into the account after all those failed attempts. Luckily, no one did!

Why does this matter? Well, it's important to keep our digital world safe. If someone gets into a computer account, they could cause trouble or steal important information. By finding and stopping these attempts early, we can protect our computers and the information on them.
