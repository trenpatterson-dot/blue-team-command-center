# SOC-018 OneNote Notes

## Project
SOC-018 — Microsoft Sentinel Alert Investigation

## What I Practiced
- Reviewing a Sentinel-style sign-in alert
- Building an investigation timeline
- Checking authentication results
- Reviewing MFA status
- Mapping activity to MITRE ATT&CK
- Writing a final analyst disposition

## Main Lesson
A suspicious alert is not automatically a confirmed compromise. The analyst has to review evidence, look for follow-on activity, and explain the final decision clearly.
