## Project
SOC-019 — Microsoft Defender XDR Investigation

## What I Practiced
- Reviewing a Defender XDR endpoint alert
- Checking process behavior
- Reviewing parent and child processes
- Analyzing suspicious PowerShell activity
- Mapping evidence to MITRE ATT&CK
- Writing a final analyst disposition

## Main Lesson
PowerShell activity is not automatically malicious. The analyst has to review context, parent process, command-line behavior, and follow-on activity before deciding.
