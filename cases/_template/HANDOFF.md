\## Safe Handoff Summary



Write a short plain-English handoff.



Example:



> This case is still active. Current evidence shows suspicious activity, but compromise has not been confirmed. The next analyst should review the missing context, verify whether activity is expected, and escalate only if additional evidence supports it.



\## Final Reminder



Before handing off:



\- \[ ] Facts are separated from assumptions

\- \[ ] Missing information is clear

\- \[ ] Evidence location is documented safely

\- \[ ] Approval-required actions are marked

\- \[ ] Sensitive data is protected

\- \[ ] The next analyst can continue without guessing
