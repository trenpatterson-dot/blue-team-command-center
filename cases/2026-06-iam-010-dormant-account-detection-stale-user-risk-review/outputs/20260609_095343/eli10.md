# Cybersecurity Lab: Dormant Account Detection

In this lab, we were on a mission to find out about dormant accounts that might be risky for our computer system. This is called **Dormant Account Detection Stale User Risk Review**. It's like cleaning up an old toy box to make sure everything is still useful and safe.

Here's what we did:
1. We looked at a list of user accounts on the system. This was like checking out all the toys in our toy box.
2. For each account, we checked when it was last used (logged in or signed in). If it had been a long time since an account was used, we marked it as potentially dormant.
3. We also looked at what groups each account belongs to and if they have special privileges. This helps us understand if the account is important or not.

What we found:
1. We discovered some accounts that haven't been used for a long time. These could be dormant and might need our attention.
2. Some accounts still had access to important parts of the system. We made notes about these accounts so we can keep an eye on them.

Why it matters:
Dormant accounts can be a problem because they might have old passwords that are easy for bad guys to guess. Also, if someone gains control of a dormant account, they could cause trouble in our system. By finding and managing these accounts, we're keeping our computer system safe and secure.
