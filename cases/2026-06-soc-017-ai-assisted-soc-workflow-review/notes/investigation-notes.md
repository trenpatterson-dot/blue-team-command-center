# SOC-017 Investigation Notes

Finding 1:
The alert combined identity activity and endpoint activity.

Finding 2:
AI assistance was useful for summarizing the alert and organizing evidence.

Finding 3:
Human validation was required because AI could overstate the conclusion.

Finding 4:
The evidence supports suspicious activity and escalation.

Finding 5:
The evidence does not confirm successful compromise or data loss.

Analyst Decision:
Escalate for endpoint review and identity verification.

Responsible AI Lesson:
AI can speed up documentation, but the analyst must validate evidence and own the final decision.
