## Scenario
A SOC analyst uses AI assistance to help summarize a suspicious security alert, create an evidence checklist, suggest MITRE ATT&CK mapping, and draft an incident report. The analyst then validates the AI output, corrects unsupported claims, documents limitations, and makes the final human-approved determination.

## Analyst Goal
Demonstrate responsible AI-assisted SOC work while keeping the final security decision under human analyst control.

## Evidence To Review
- Security alert summary
- Correlated event evidence
- KQL-style review query
- AI-assisted summary draft
- Analyst validation checklist
- AI limitations note
- Incident report draft
- Final analyst determination
- Investigation notes
- Screenshot evidence

## Supervisor Instruction
Generate documentation only from the provided evidence. Treat this as a simulated AI-assisted SOC workflow case. Do not claim that AI made the final security decision. Make clear that the human analyst validated the evidence and approved the final determination.
