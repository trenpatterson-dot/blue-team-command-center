# MFA Review

## Objective

Review authentication controls and identify risks associated with weak or missing MFA protections.

## Observations

* Privileged accounts represent the highest risk if compromised.
* MFA significantly reduces the likelihood of successful credential attacks.
* Identity-based attacks commonly target administrative accounts.

## Risks

* Credential theft
* Password spraying
* Phishing attacks
* Credential reuse

## Recommendations

* Require MFA for all privileged accounts.
* Require MFA for remote access.
* Require MFA for access to sensitive business applications.
* Review MFA exclusions quarterly.

## Risk Rating

High
