# LinkedIn Draft: Azure Conditional Access / MFA Risk Review

I completed a documentation-first Microsoft Azure identity-security case study focused on Conditional Access and MFA risk review.

The simulated review covered:

- Conditional Access policy scope and enforcement
- MFA registration compared with actual sign-in requirements
- Privileged-role protection
- Excluded users and groups
- Risky sign-in controls
- Legacy authentication exposure
- Break-glass account monitoring
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping for identity attack paths

My analyst recommendations prioritized validating privileged-role coverage, reducing broad exclusions, testing risk-based controls in report-only mode, retiring legacy authentication through controlled migration, and alerting on emergency access use.

The main lesson was that broad MFA adoption does not automatically mean consistent protection. Policy scope, exceptions, risk signals, authentication methods, monitoring, testing, and human approval all matter.

This was a simulated lab-based portfolio case. It did not involve a production tenant, employer environment, customer data, or live remediation.
