# v0.3 Workplan

## Goal

Expand Blue Team Command Center with more SOC investigation workflows and stronger analyst-review structure.

Blue Team Command Center remains a portable Markdown-first SOC and Blue Team analyst workbench. v0.3 builds on the stable v0.2.1 release without weakening the public/private boundary, human approval safeguards, or review-only posture for operational actions.

- Human approval safeguards
- Markdown rendering cleanup

- No real company evidence.
