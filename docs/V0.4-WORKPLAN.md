Expand Blue Team Command Center with vulnerability finding and endpoint alert triage workflows.

Blue Team Command Center remains a portable Markdown-first SOC and Blue Team analyst workbench. v0.4 builds on the stable v0.3.1 release without weakening the public/private boundary, human approval safeguards, or review-only posture for operational actions.

- Analyst reports

- Lessons learned reports

- Public/private evidence boundary
- Human approval safeguards

## Safety Rules

- No secrets.
- No real company evidence.
- No screenshots unless sanitized and reviewed.
- No destructive action.
- No live scanning unless explicitly approved.
- No exploitation instructions.
- Human approval required for operational actions.
