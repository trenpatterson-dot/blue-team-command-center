# Report Template Library

Use these templates as starting points. Copy the template into a case folder or approved reporting location, then fill in only confirmed information.

## Templates

- `templates/ALERT-TRIAGE-REPORT.md`
- `templates/INCIDENT-REPORT.md`
- `templates/EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md`
- `templates/LESSONS-LEARNED.md`
- `templates/DETECTION-ENGINEERING-REPORT.md`
- `templates/THREAT-HUNTING-REPORT.md`
- `templates/PHISHING-ANALYSIS-REPORT.md`
- `templates/VULNERABILITY-REMEDIATION-REPORT.md`
- `templates/AUDIT-FINDING-REPORT.md`

## Standard Sections

Every report separates:

- Known facts.
- Analyst interpretation.
- Assumptions.
- Missing information.
- Recommended next steps.
- Human approval required.

## Use Guidance

- Keep facts traceable to approved sources.
- Avoid overstating confidence.
- Make uncertainty visible.
- Do not include secrets or restricted data.
- Sanitize before sharing outside the approved audience.
