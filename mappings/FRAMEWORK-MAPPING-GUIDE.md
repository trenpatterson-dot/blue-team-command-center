# Framework Mapping Guide

Framework mappings help explain why an investigation, control, or recommendation matters. They do not prove compliance by themselves.

## How to Use

1. Start with the case facts.
2. Identify the activity type, such as detection, response, recovery, access control, vulnerability management, or training.
3. Choose the closest mapping file.
4. Cite the relevant framework area at a high level.
5. Avoid claiming certification, compliance, or maturity unless validated by an authorized audit process.

## Reporting Language

Use cautious wording:

- "This activity supports..."
- "This finding relates to..."
- "This recommendation aligns with..."

Avoid unsupported wording:

- "This proves compliance."
- "This closes the control."
- "This meets all requirements."
