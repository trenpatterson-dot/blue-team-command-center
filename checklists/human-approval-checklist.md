# Human Approval Checklist

Use this checklist before any operational action.

- [ ] The proposed action is clearly written.
- [ ] The expected benefit is documented.
- [ ] The risk of taking the action is documented.
- [ ] The risk of not taking the action is documented.
- [ ] The affected users, systems, or business processes are identified.
- [ ] The approval owner has authority for the action.
- [ ] Approval was granted in an approved system or channel.
- [ ] The action will be performed by an authorized person or approved process.
- [ ] Rollback or recovery considerations are documented when applicable.
