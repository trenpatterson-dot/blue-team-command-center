# Evidence Handling Checklist

- [ ] Evidence is stored in the approved system of record.
- [ ] Local copies are permitted by policy, if any exist.
- [ ] No secrets, credentials, tokens, or private keys are stored here.
- [ ] Sensitive identifiers are minimized or referenced by approved IDs.
- [ ] Known facts are traceable to approved sources.
- [ ] Analyst interpretation is clearly labeled.
- [ ] Assumptions are clearly labeled.
- [ ] Missing information is documented.
- [ ] Screenshot references use names without `.png`, such as `identity-alert-01`.
- [ ] Evidence has not been modified, deleted, or moved without approval.
