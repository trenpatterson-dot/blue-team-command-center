# Phishing Triage Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing a suspicious email, reported phishing message, or email security alert.

The goal is to determine whether the message is benign, suspicious, malicious, or needs escalation while protecting users and evidence.

## 1. Case Basics

- [ ] Case or ticket created
- [ ] Reporter documented, if allowed
- [ ] Alert/source documented
- [ ] Analyst documented
- [ ] Date and time documented
- [ ] Timezone documented
- [ ] Severity documented
- [ ] Related playbook linked
