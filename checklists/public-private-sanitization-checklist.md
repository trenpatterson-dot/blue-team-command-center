# Public Private Sanitization Checklist

Use this before sharing material outside the approved audience.

- [ ] Company name is removed unless approved.
- [ ] Customer, employee, and third-party names are removed unless approved.
- [ ] Usernames, emails, hostnames, IPs, domains, URLs, and ticket IDs are removed or generalized unless approved.
- [ ] Screenshots are removed or replaced with approved sanitized examples.
- [ ] Logs and raw evidence are removed.
- [ ] Tool names are removed when policy requires it.
- [ ] Dates and timelines are generalized when needed.
- [ ] Secrets, credentials, tokens, and keys are not present.
- [ ] The final version has been reviewed by the correct owner before sharing.
