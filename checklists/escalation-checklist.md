# Escalation Checklist

- [ ] Severity appears appropriate for current facts.
- [ ] Impacted users, hosts, systems, and data are identified as far as permitted.
- [ ] The business owner or incident owner is known.
- [ ] Escalation reason is written plainly.
- [ ] Known facts are separated from interpretation.
- [ ] Missing information is listed.
- [ ] Recommended next steps are specific.
- [ ] Human approval requirements are listed.
- [ ] Official case or ticket reference is included.
