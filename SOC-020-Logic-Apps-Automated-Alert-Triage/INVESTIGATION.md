## Findings

PowerShell operational logs were reviewed for evidence of suspicious execution activity.

Event IDs 400, 403, and 600 were observed and represented normal PowerShell engine startup and shutdown activity.

A review of running processes identified common user applications including ChatGPT, Chrome, Spotify, Google Drive, and Windows system processes.

No evidence of malicious PowerShell execution, persistence mechanisms, or suspicious processes was identified.

The alert was determined to be a false positive generated from normal PowerShell activity.

The incident was closed as informational.
